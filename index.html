About Me

I'm an IT graduate from NIT Kurukshetra (May 2026) who doesn't just study systems — I build, debug and deploy them. My focus is real-time, production-grade web applications and autonomous multi-agent AI systems that real people and real workflows depend on. My most significant work is Thought Lab Portal — a full-stack platform I architected, built, and deployed as a freelance engagement. I've competed at Flipkart GRiD 7.0 (National Semi-Finalist), led a college SIH team to victory, and ranked top 7 at Excalibur out of 60 teams.

I think like a product engineer: design → build → deploy → iterate. I'm available to join immediately — whether it's a real-time system that needs to scale or an agentic workflow that needs to run autonomously, if you need someone who ships, let's talk.
